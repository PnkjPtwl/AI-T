# Enterprise Sales Playbook

## Discovery

Understand

- Customer goals
- Existing systems
- Business challenges
- Current vendors

## Qualification

Evaluate

- Budget
- Timeline
- Decision makers
- Technical readiness

## Value Selling

Focus on

- ROI
- Automation
- Operational efficiency
- AI adoption
- Cost reduction

## Common Objections

### Budget

Highlight ROI and phased implementation.

### Security

Explain compliance certifications and security architecture.

### Integration

Describe APIs and implementation methodology.

### AI Accuracy

Demonstrate real customer outcomes and pilot approach.

## Closing Strategy

- Executive alignment
- Pilot project
- Success criteria
- Expansion roadmap
