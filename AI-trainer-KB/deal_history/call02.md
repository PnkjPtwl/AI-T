# Call 02 – Technical Requirements Workshop

## Meeting Objective

Understand Phoenix Automotive's technical requirements for the upcoming electric vehicle program, discuss manufacturing challenges, and determine how Relanto's automotive products and AI-powered manufacturing solutions could support the company's modernization initiatives.

---

## Transcript

**Rahul (Relanto):**
Hi Sarah. Thank you for organizing today's workshop. I understand you've had a chance to review the material we shared after our last discussion.

**Sarah (Phoenix):**
Yes, we did. Our engineering team reviewed your brake system portfolio and your AI quality inspection solution. Both look promising, and we'd like to understand how they would fit into our manufacturing environment.

**Rahul:**
Absolutely. I've invited Arjun from our solutions engineering team to walk through the technical aspects and answer any detailed questions.

**Arjun (Relanto):**
Hi Sarah. To recommend the right solution, we'd first like to understand your current production process. Could you describe how quality inspections are currently performed?

**Sarah:**
Most inspections are still manual. Operators perform visual checks at different production stages, and although we have inspection guidelines, defects are sometimes identified only during final assembly. That increases rework and occasionally affects delivery schedules.

**Arjun:**
Understood. Are inspection images or production data currently stored in a centralized system?

**Sarah:**
Only partially. Production data is available through our MES platform, while inspection reports are maintained separately. We don't currently have AI-based defect detection or predictive analytics.

**Rahul:**
Besides manufacturing quality, are you also reviewing your component suppliers for the new vehicle platform?

**Sarah:**
Yes. We're evaluating brake pads, brake discs, and ABS sensor suppliers for the upcoming SUV program. Product quality, engineering support, and supplier responsiveness are more important to us than simply selecting the lowest-cost vendor.

**Arjun:**
That's helpful. Our brake systems are manufactured under IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 standards, and our engineering team regularly supports OEM customization projects. On the AI side, our platform can integrate with existing manufacturing systems to monitor inspection data and identify quality issues much earlier in the production cycle.

**Sarah:**
Integration is an important factor for us. We already use SAP S/4HANA along with our MES platform. We would prefer a solution that integrates with our existing systems rather than replacing them.

**Arjun:**
That's exactly how our platform is designed. We focus on integrating with existing manufacturing environments, allowing customers to continue using their ERP and MES systems while adding intelligent analytics and AI-powered inspection capabilities.

**Sarah:**
That's good to hear. Our leadership team is particularly interested in reducing warranty claims and improving production visibility before the new vehicle program begins.

**Rahul:**
Based on today's discussion, I believe there are two areas where we can support Phoenix. The first is supplying automotive brake components for your new platform, and the second is implementing our AI Quality Inspection Platform to strengthen manufacturing quality.

**Sarah:**
I agree. The next step would be to see a detailed demonstration of both solutions, especially the manufacturing analytics platform.

**Rahul:**
Perfect. We'll arrange a product demonstration and prepare a preliminary commercial proposal so your team can begin evaluating both opportunities.

**Sarah:**
Sounds good. I'll coordinate with our engineering and operations teams so they can attend the next session.

# Meeting Summary

The workshop focused on Phoenix Automotive's manufacturing environment, supplier evaluation process, and digital transformation goals. The discussion confirmed that Phoenix is actively searching for a strategic supplier capable of providing both automotive components and intelligent manufacturing solutions. The technical team expressed particular interest in AI-powered quality inspection, predictive analytics, and seamless integration with existing SAP ERP and MES platforms.

---

# Key Business Requirements Identified

- High-quality brake pads, brake discs, and ABS sensors.
- AI-powered visual quality inspection.
- Predictive manufacturing analytics.
- Integration with SAP S/4HANA and existing MES systems.
- Reduced warranty claims and production rework.
- Strong engineering collaboration and long-term supplier support.
