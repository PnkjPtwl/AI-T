# Call 03 – Product Demonstration & Proposal Discussion

## Meeting Objective

Present Relanto's automotive product portfolio and AI Quality Inspection Platform, address technical questions, discuss commercial expectations, and review the next steps for both active opportunities.

---

## Transcript

**Rahul (Relanto):**

Hi Sarah, thank you for joining again. Based on our previous discussions, we've prepared a demonstration covering both the brake system modernization proposal and the AI Quality Inspection Platform. We'd also like to walk you through our initial commercial proposal.

---

**Sarah (Phoenix):**

Great. Our engineering team reviewed the technical documents you shared last week. Overall the feedback was positive, especially around the quality certifications and manufacturing capabilities. Today we'd like to understand how your solutions would fit into our production environment and whether the commercial proposal aligns with our budget.

---

**Arjun (Relanto):**

Absolutely. Let me begin with the AI platform.

The platform connects directly with existing production systems and inspection stations. Rather than replacing your MES or SAP environment, it continuously analyzes production data, inspection images, and quality metrics to identify defects earlier in the manufacturing process.

Managers receive real-time dashboards showing defect trends, production bottlenecks, and quality KPIs while engineers receive recommendations for potential root causes.

---

**Sarah:**

That integration approach is actually one of the reasons we're interested. We don't want another isolated application that requires duplicate data entry.

Can your platform integrate with SAP S/4HANA without major customization?

---

**Arjun:**

Yes.

Our standard implementation integrates with SAP and MES through APIs, allowing production data and inspection results to remain synchronized. Most deployments require configuration rather than significant custom development.

---

**Rahul:**

Alongside the AI platform, we've also prepared a proposal for supplying brake pads, brake discs, and ABS sensors for your new electric SUV program.

Our engineering team believes we can support your production schedule while improving product consistency and reducing warranty-related issues.

---

**Sarah:**

The proposal looks promising.

From a technical perspective I don't see any major concerns.

However, procurement will likely ask about pricing before moving forward. We're also comparing your proposal with Bosch since they're our current supplier.

---

**Rahul:**

That's understandable.

Besides pricing, are there any specific areas where Bosch currently has an advantage?

---

**Sarah:**

Mostly familiarity.

They've worked with us for several years, so changing suppliers naturally introduces some risk. Your technical support and AI capabilities are definitely stronger, but the commercial team will compare overall cost before making a recommendation.

---

**Rahul:**

That makes sense.

We believe our value comes from more than component pricing. Faster engineering support, AI-driven quality improvements, lower warranty costs, and better operational visibility should deliver measurable savings over the life of the program.

We're happy to work with your procurement team if they'd like a more detailed ROI analysis.

---

**Sarah:**

I think that would be useful.

I'd also like a breakdown of implementation activities for the AI platform and a revised commercial proposal showing available pricing options.

---

**Rahul:**

Absolutely.

We'll prepare a revised proposal, implementation roadmap, estimated ROI, and deployment timeline before our next meeting.

Once procurement has reviewed the commercial proposal, we can schedule an executive discussion with your leadership team.

---

**Sarah:**

Perfect.

I'll share today's discussion with procurement and operations. Assuming there aren't any major commercial issues, we should be ready for executive review shortly.

---

**Rahul:**

Excellent.

Thanks again for your time today. We'll follow up with the revised proposal and supporting documentation within the next few days.
