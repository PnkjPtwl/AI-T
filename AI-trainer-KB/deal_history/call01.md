# Call 01 – Discovery & Qualification

## Meeting Objective

Conduct an introductory discussion to understand Phoenix Automotive's current manufacturing environment, supplier landscape, business objectives, and determine whether Relanto's automotive solutions could address the company's operational challenges.

---

## Transcript

**Rahul (Relanto):**
Hi Sarah, it's great to finally meet you. Thank you for taking the time today. Before we discuss our solutions, I'd like to understand a little more about Phoenix Automotive and the challenges your team is currently facing.

**Sarah (Phoenix):**
Thanks, Rahul. We've been evaluating several suppliers as part of our manufacturing modernization initiative. We're preparing for a new electric SUV program, and we're reviewing both our component suppliers and some AI-based manufacturing technologies.

**Rahul:**
That's exciting. Could you tell me what's driving this supplier evaluation?

**Sarah:**
Our current suppliers have generally performed well, but we've started seeing longer delivery timelines and higher component costs. We're also looking for partners who can provide better engineering collaboration instead of simply supplying products.

**Rahul:**
Understood. Besides supplier performance, are there any operational challenges within manufacturing that you're trying to improve?

**Sarah:**
Yes. Our quality inspection process still relies heavily on manual reviews. We occasionally identify defects later in production than we'd like, which increases rework and warranty costs. Management wants us to explore AI-based inspection and predictive quality monitoring.

**Rahul:**
That aligns well with several projects we've delivered for automotive manufacturers. Are you mainly focused on braking systems, or are you evaluating broader manufacturing improvements as well?

**Sarah:**
Initially we're reviewing braking components for the new vehicle platform, but if we identify the right technology partner, we'd also like to evaluate AI solutions that can improve production quality and operational visibility.

**Rahul:**
That makes sense. What would success look like from your perspective over the next six to twelve months?

**Sarah:**
We're looking for a supplier who can consistently deliver high-quality components, support engineering discussions quickly, and help us reduce manufacturing defects before our new vehicle launch.

**Rahul:**
Perfect. I think there are several areas where we can help. Rather than discussing products today, I'd suggest arranging a technical workshop with both engineering teams so we can better understand your specifications and recommend the right approach.

**Sarah:**
That sounds reasonable. I'll coordinate with our engineering leads and schedule a follow-up session next week.

**Rahul:**
Excellent. I'll also send over a brief overview of our automotive portfolio before the workshop so your team has some background material.

**Sarah:**
Great. Looking forward to the next discussion.

---

# Meeting Summary

The call focused on understanding Phoenix Automotive's current business objectives, supplier challenges, and manufacturing priorities. Sarah explained that Phoenix is evaluating strategic suppliers for its upcoming electric vehicle program while also exploring AI-driven manufacturing technologies. Relanto identified a strong opportunity to support both automotive component requirements and manufacturing modernization initiatives.

- Rising procurement costs
- Manual quality inspection processes
- Higher warranty and rework costs
- Need for stronger engineering collaboration
- Interest in AI-driven manufacturing improvements
